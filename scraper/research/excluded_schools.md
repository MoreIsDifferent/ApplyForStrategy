# Schools excluded from `schools.yaml`

Schools from `school_rankings.csv` that, after directory research in Task 3, had no
findable Strategy-area faculty directory page and were therefore not added to
`schools.yaml` (Stanford-GSB-style exclusion).

| Slug | Name | Reason |
|------|------|--------|
| stanford-gsb | Stanford GSB | No unified Strategy department; the GSB's academic areas are Accounting, Economics, Finance, Marketing, Operations Information & Technology, Organizational Behavior, and Political Economy. Strategy-adjacent faculty are split across Organizational Behavior and Political Economy with no combined Strategy roster page. |
| johns-hopkins-carey | Johns Hopkins Carey | Carey Business School is organized around cross-cutting programs (e.g. MS in Management, analytics, finance) rather than traditional academic departments; no Strategy or Management department roster page exists. The general faculty directory (carey.jhu.edu/faculty/faculty-directory) has no department/area filter and is also bot-protected (403 to automated fetches). |
